Do you charge buyers a fee?

Our brokerage is discussed upfront and in writing before any visit — no surprises at registry.

Can you help with a home loan?

Yes. We work with leading banks and housing-finance companies to get you pre-approved before you shortlist.

How do you verify a property?

Title search, RERA registration check, approved plan match and a physical visit — before the listing ever reaches you.

I want to sell. How fast can you list?

Usually within 48 hours, including a pricing opinion based on recent deals in your society and locality.

Do you handle commercial property?

Offices, showrooms and shops — buying, selling and leasing across Vadodara's main corridors.
